[h1]Auto-Cut Blight (rimhunter)[/h1]

Blighted plants are marked for cutting on their own, and your colonists go and cut them first. Works on RimWorld 1.6 and 1.7 from one subscription.

This is a new mod, written from scratch by rimhunter. The idea is from the original Auto-Cut Blight. No code or asset of any build of the original was used, because the original has no license, and none of its builds was opened.

[h2]What it does[/h2]

[list]
[*]A plant that becomes blighted on a player home map gets a plant-cut order. No click is needed.
[*]Blight that is already on a home map when you add the mod also gets its orders.
[*]A colonist whose plant cutting priority is at or above their best ordinary work priority takes blighted plants first, ahead of any further sowing or ordinary cutting.
[*]If you cancel an order, it stays canceled, also after a save and a load.
[*]A plant that already has an order keeps it. A plant that gets blighted again gets a new order.
[/list]

[h2]What it does not do[/h2]

[list]
[*]It has no settings.
[*]It shows no letter.
[*]The mod adds no interruption of its own: it is an emergency work giver, and the game decides when a colonist picks a new job. In the tests the colonist cut after the sow job in hand ended.
[*]Its text is in English only.
[*]It does not change vanilla blight or the Cut all blight button.
[*]It needs no Harmony and no other mod.
[/list]

[h2]What was tested[/h2]

Every in-game test of the mod passed on RimWorld 1.7.5014 and on 1.6.4871, and each one fails when it is run without the mod. Some of those checks fail because the mod is missing rather than because of the behavior; the record says which. Each test ran once per version, except one that failed in 3 of 17 earlier runs and then passed 6 of 6 on each version. There is no picture of a blighted plant carrying its order, and the original was never run. The full record is in the repository: [url=https://github.com/rimhunter/auto-cut-blight/tree/main/docs/verification]https://github.com/rimhunter/auto-cut-blight/tree/main/docs/verification[/url]
